Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can outrun a small heater quickly, and a high-temperature dish machine needs a booster to reach its sanitizing temperature. Norman Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep the kitchen and restrooms supplied, and we plan visits around your operating hours.

Staff can't run the dish room without hot water that reaches the right temperature, and inspectors check the handwashing sinks fast. Our service keeps temperatures and recovery where your kitchen needs them, backed by written reports and service logs after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to inspectors and landlords.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and downtime stays short so the kitchen opens on time.

Code & Inspections

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect venting and draft, confirm proper combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

After-Hours Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens can't sanitize, salons turn clients away, hotel guests complain, and tenants start calling. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling fast for emergency repairs throughout Norman and the surrounding area, and planned work can be booked overnight or before opening to limit downtime.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on commercial equipment. The priority is diagnosing the fault and getting hot water back as quickly as possible, and in a multi-heater system we isolate the failed unit so the others carry the load. After that, we put maintenance in place to prevent a repeat, and every response is fully documented for your records.

Preventive Care

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Norman Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and verification of delivered water temperature.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses on a regular schedule typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and more consistent water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
