How Much Does Water Heater Repair Cost in Norman?

The typical water heater repair in Norman costs $150 to $600. The part that failed drives the price: an element or thermocouple is a quick, inexpensive fix, while a gas control valve is a bigger job. You'll get an upfront quote from Norman Water Heater before we touch a single part.

Factors That Change the Price

  • Fuel type, because gas valves and burner parts cost more than electric elements
  • The age and brand of the heater and how easy its parts are to source
  • Whether the repair is urgent, after hours, or can be scheduled
